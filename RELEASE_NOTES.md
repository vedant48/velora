## One settings icon, new Settings screen

- The gear and the three-dot button on your profile are now one settings icon.
- Settings now follows the new design: your profile card on top (tap it to edit photo, name and bio), then Appearance, Private account, Import from Must, Export your data, Check for updates, Share profile, Activity, About & Credits, Log out and Delete account.
- Everything the three-dot menu had is in Settings now.
- Settings no longer draws behind the status bar.
- Still in: header cut at the status bar, no blank gap on tab switch, one-line name, "Member since" line, original Velora V logo.

No change to your account or library; update in place.
